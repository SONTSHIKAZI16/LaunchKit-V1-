# Mthiya LaunchKit V1 — Build #022: Go-To-Market Builder

Build #022 converts the business plan, financial constraints, offer and customer evidence into a focused first-launch motion.

Forecasts and goals remain hypotheses; the AI is instructed not to invent demand, conversion rates, traction or market facts.
